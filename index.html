A FEW QUICK NOTES

Making a wish, made easy.

Can I make the message feel more personal?

Yes. Add one thing you love about them and a favourite memory. Both details become part of the birthday note.

How do I send the wish to them?

Once the wish appears, choose “Copy this wish” to paste it anywhere, or choose “Share the love” to open your device's sharing options.

Can I make a wish for someone else?

Of course. Go back to the form, enter a different name, choose how you know them, and create a fresh note.
